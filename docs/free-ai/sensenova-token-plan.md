# SenseNova Token Plan (Free Beta)

Free open beta: the general-credit pool and the Flash-Lite credit pool each provide a limited-time supply of 60,000 credits per rolling 5 hours and 600,000 credits per rolling week; Flash-Lite usage o…

SenseNova Token Plan is SenseTime's free open-beta program for its SenseNova (日日新) large-model platform. During the beta, models opened on the platform (e.g. DeepSeek V4 Flash, SenseNova 6.7 Flash-Lite, SenseNova U1 Fast; the list may expand or change) are free through the OpenAI-compatible gateway (https://token.sensenova.cn/v1), and integrations such as WorkBuddy and Claude Code style tools are supported. From 2026-08-28 the platform applies a credits system: accounts carry general credits (shared by all opened models) and Flash-Lite-specific credits (Flash-Lite family only); during the beta each pool provides a rolling 60,000 credits per 5 hours and 600,000 credits per week. A rebate activity also runs from 2026-08-28: every Flash-Lite-specific credit consumed earns 1 general credit back (aggregated by day, settled hourly; rebated credits are valid 30 days and do not count against the rolling limits). Beta benefits and activities may change; confirm on the platform page before relying on them.
