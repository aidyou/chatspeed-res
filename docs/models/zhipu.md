# Zhipu GLM

Zhipu AI's MaaS platform with a permanently free GLM Flash tier (GLM-4-Flash / GLM-4.7-Flash) plus sign-up credits for paid GLM models.

Zhipu AI (智谱) offers GLM models through an OpenAI-compatible MaaS endpoint. The GLM-4-Flash text model is announced as permanently free without call-volume restrictions, and the newer GLM-4.7-Flash is also free; GLM-4V-Flash is a free image-understanding model. New accounts get learning credits for paid GLM models (community reports around 20M tokens; check the official console). Free-tier concurrency is generous but check the official rate-limit doc for current values.
