# SiliconFlow

An OpenAI-compatible inference platform with free sign-up credit (about ¥14 / 20M tokens) and many permanently free open models after real-name verification.

SiliconFlow (硅基流动) hosts 200+ open models (DeepSeek, Qwen, Llama, Kimi, MiniMax, GLM etc.) behind one OpenAI-compatible endpoint. New users receive a small credit grant (about ¥14, roughly 20M tokens of Qwen-class models) and can use permanently free model variants after real-name verification; free variants have fixed rate limits and cost 0. The endpoint can be connected to ChatSpeed.
