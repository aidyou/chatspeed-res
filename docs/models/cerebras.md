# Cerebras

OpenAI-compatible wafer-scale inference with a free tier of roughly 1M tokens/day (e.g. Llama 3.3 70B), no credit card required.

Cerebras hosts open LLMs on its wafer-scale hardware at very high throughput (up to ~2,000 tokens/s on some models) behind an OpenAI-compatible API. The free tier provides roughly 1M tokens/day shared per model, with per-model RPM/TPM/TPD limits and no credit card required; exceeding limits returns 429. Paid Developer tier offers ~10x higher limits. Check the official rate-limits doc for current values.
