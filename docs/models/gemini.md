# Google Gemini

Google's Gemini API with a free tier covering Flash / Flash-Lite models (free input & output tokens), generous quotas for light daily use, and no credit card needed for the free tier.

The Gemini API offers a free tier for developers and small projects. Free-tier-eligible models include the Flash and Flash-Lite families (e.g. Gemini 2.5 Flash, Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite, newer 3.x Flash Lite variants), with free input/output tokens and relatively generous RPM/RPD quotas that are shown in Google AI Studio and change over time. The API is available natively and through an OpenAI-compatible endpoint; the free tier requires a Google account but no billing/credit card.
